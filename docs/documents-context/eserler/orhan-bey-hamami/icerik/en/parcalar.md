# Orhan Bey Bath · 3D part cards (English)

Translation of `../parcalar.md`.

| Parçalar | Title (EN) | Text (EN) |
|---|---|---|
| Hamam_Giris | Entrances | The building, known today as the "Old Aynalı Bazaar", has three entrances: north, south and west. According to the inventory the northern entrance is original and the others were opened later. With the repairs after the 1958 Bazaar fire the bath was converted entirely into a bazaar; the furnace room was demolished and the bazaar's west entrance opened. |
| Hamam_Sogukluk_Erkek | Men's cool room | The main northern entrance leads into the square cool room of the men's section, 11.95 × 11.86 metres. It is covered by a dome on an octagonal drum reached through squinches. Today the interior is divided with wood and steel and used as rows of shops, mostly selling antiques and souvenirs. |
| Hamam_Sicaklik | Warm room, hot room and private cells | In the original plan, the hot room of the men's section had four private cells at the corners, three iwans with benches and a heated central stone. The cells are domed and the iwans vaulted; the whole space takes light from windows in the dome drums and from skylights above the domes. All the bath's domes and vaults are covered with lead. |
| Hamam_Sogukluk_Kadin, Hamam_Giris_Kadin | Women's section | The women's section, adjoining the men's on the east, begins with a square cool room of 10.35 × 10.35 metres, covered by a dome on an octagonal drum and lit by a skylight. Archival research showed the building to be a double bath; the women's section was rebuilt in 1943 in work led by the architect Sedat Çetintaş. After the restoration an entrance reached by stairs was opened on the south. |
