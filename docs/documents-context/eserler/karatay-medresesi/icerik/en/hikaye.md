# Karatay Madrasa · Story (English)

Translation of `../hikaye.md`. Sources and trust labels come from the Turkish record.

| # | Sentence (EN) |
|---|---|
| 1 | According to the inscription on its portal, the Karatay Madrasa was built in 1251. |
| 2 | Its founder, Emir Celaleddin Karatay, set it directly across from the Kemaliye Madrasa built by his elder brother. |
| 3 | Its endowment deed of 1253–54 required the teacher to be of the Hanafi school; there was no such requirement for students. |
| 4 | According to the deed, lessons were held five days a week, with Tuesday and Friday off. |
| 5 | The decoration of white and grey marble on the portal shows Syrian influence. |
| 6 | The closed courtyard is covered by a dome 12 metres across, with a skylight at its centre. |
| 7 | The interior is decorated with mosaic, openwork and plate tiles. |
| 8 | The building has been used as the Museum of Tile Works since 1955. |
