# Emir Han · 3D part cards (English)

Translation of `../parcalar.md`.

| Parçalar | Title (EN) | Text (EN) |
|---|---|---|
| Emir_Hani | The han and its rooms | The two-storey han of about 45 × 45 metres has 36 rooms on the lower floor and 38 on the upper. The windowless lower rooms were stores protecting goods brought by caravans; the upper ones, with hearths, lodged the merchants. The walls alternate two courses of brick with one of küfeki stone. Its roof is covered with lead today; it is known to have been tiled for a time. |
| Girisler | Entrances and weighing benches | The han has two entrances, one from the Ulucami courtyard and the other from the Covered Bazaar. Goods bought and sold in the city were weighed here; the benches built for the scales used from the conquest of Bursa until 1781 can still be seen at the entrance. Because the bazaar's scales were here, the han was also known as "Bahar Kapanı". |
| Avlu_Revaklari | Arcades | The courtyard is surrounded by arcades on wide piers of cut stone and brick. In the lower arcade of the south wing the arches are semicircular; elsewhere and on the upper floor they are pointed, and the south arcade's piers, unlike the others, are of cut stone. Today booksellers, sellers of religious goods and jewellers occupy the upper floor, with places to eat and drink below. |
| Han_Avlu, Sadirvan | Courtyard and fountain | The central courtyard, surrounded by arcades, covers about 650 square metres. With its jet fountain and the shade of historic plane trees, the han is still a meeting place for conversation over tea and coffee. |
