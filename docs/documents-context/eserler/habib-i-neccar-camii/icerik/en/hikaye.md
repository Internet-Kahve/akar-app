# Habib-i Neccar Mosque · Story (English)

Translation of `../hikaye.md`. Sources and trust labels come from the Turkish record.

| # | Sentence (EN) |
|---|---|
| 1 | According to one account, Antakya is where Islam began to spread in Anatolia, and the first mosque built in Anatolia is here: Habib-i Neccar Mosque. |
| 2 | Habib-i Neccar, who gives the mosque its name, is a martyr praised in the Surah Yâsin; according to the book, his grave is inside the mosque. |
| 3 | The oldest surviving inscription bears the title of Baybars, the Mamluk sultan who freed the city from Mongol rule in 1268. |
| 4 | The mosque has come down to us through repairs under the Mamluks in 1474 and the Ottomans in 1721, 1858 and 1863. |
| 5 | In the trapezoidal courtyard the mosque lies to the south, madrasa rooms around it and a fountain to the north. |
| 6 | With its six-bay portico and twenty-four-sided minaret, the mosque is considered the representative of classical Ottoman taste in Antakya. |
| 7 | The four-line construction inscription of the courtyard fountain is dated 1858. |
| 8 | The mosque was badly damaged in the earthquakes of 6 February 2023; its minaret and portico collapsed, and rescue and repair work has begun. |
