# Bedesten · 3D part cards (English)

Translation of `../parcalar.md`.

| Parçalar | Title (EN) | Text (EN) |
|---|---|---|
| Govde, Ic_Duvarlar | Bedesten | The first bedesten built by the Ottomans, it was commissioned by Yıldırım Bayezid as the city rapidly turned into a centre of trade, and it became the model for every later Ottoman bedesten. Set up near Ulucami, it both provided income for the foundation and formed the core of the market district. It was built of stone and brick together. |
| Kubbeler, Govde_Ortu, Kubbe_Ici, Pandantifler | Fourteen domes | The central space, measuring 15.00 × 56.50 metres inside, is covered by fourteen domes resting on six piers. In plan the building resembles the bedestens of Istanbul and especially Edirne. |
| Payeler, Kemerler | Six piers | The fourteen domes rest on six piers lined up along the middle of the central space. The commercial spaces are shops set in front of the storerooms and around these load-bearing piers. |
| Ic_Dukkanlar | Thirty-two storerooms | The commercial spaces consist of 32 storerooms in rows running east to west and the shops in front of them, all covered by barrel vaults. The Bedesten is still largely home to jewellers' workshops today. |
| Dukkanlar | Surrounding shops | There are 68 shops around the Bedesten today, but they do not belong to the building's original layout. The sign reading "Bedesten Jewellers' Market" above one of the gates shows the building's present use. |
| Kapilar | Four gates | The Bedesten is entered through gates on its four façades; each opens into a domed entrance space. The word "Bedesten" is painted on the upper part of the west gate. |
| Kitabe | Inscription of the kalima | According to the inventory, the kalima is written in intricate kufic above the Bedesten's south gate. Ekinci's 2025 thesis places this inscription today on the west face of the pier east of the fountain, and reports its place on the south gate from older records. |
| Ic_Sokak, Sadirvan | Fountain and inner court | At the centre, placed as in a courtyard, stands a fountain with a jet. Badly damaged and left unusable by the 1958 Covered Bazaar fire, the Bedesten was extensively repaired in 1960, remaining faithful to its original architecture. |
