59’da yer almaktadır.
Koza Hanı, 1491 yılı, Ekim ayında, Sultan II. Bayezid tarafından İstanbul’da
yaptırdığı eserlerine vakıf olarak inşa edilmiştir. Yapı günümüze kadar ehemmiyetini kaybetmemiş ve işlek bir ticaret merkezi olarak kalmıştır.
The masjid is located in Osmangazi District, Sehreküstü Neighborhood, on
Uzuncarsi Street, No. 59.
The Koza Han was built in October 1491 by Sultan Bayezid II as a foundation
for his works in Istanbul. The building has not lost its importance to this day
and remains a busy trade centre.

Kozahan Pavilion Masjid is one of the rare structures that has survived to
the present day in its original form among the pavilion masjids built in the
middle of the han courtyards in Bursa. Another of these masjids is the
Fidan Han Pavilion Masjid. The masjids of Pirinç Han and Balibey Han do
not exist today.

Sekizgen planlı yapının bir yüzünde kapı, diğer yüzünde mukarnas kavsaralı
alçı mihrap bulunmaktadır. Mescit kesme taşla inşa edilmiştir ve sekiz adet
altıgen ayak üzerinde yükselen yuvarlak kemerlere oturmaktadır. Her yüzünde birer alt pencere bulunan yapının kemer aynaları şebekelidir. Mescidin
kubbesi kurşunla örtülüdür.

There is a door on one side of the octagonal structure and a plaster mihrab
with muqarnas hood on the other side. The masjid is built of cut stone and
sits on round arches rising on eight hexagonal piers. The arch panels of the
building, which has a lower window on each side, are latticed. The dome of
the masjid is covered with lead.

Yapının alt kenarlarında musluklar bulunan havuzu, on altıgen şekilli bir şadırvandır. Havuzun ortasında ise sekiz kenarlı, stalaktik başlıklı bir ayak yer
almakta, her bir yüzeyinde birer boru bulunmaktadır. Su, borular vasıtasıyla
havuza dökülmektedir. Şadırvanın, merdiven boşluğunun olduğu ve mihrabın
altına denk gelen kısımda abdest muslukları bulunmaktadır.

The pool of the building with taps on its lower edges is a hexadecagonal
shaped shadirvan. In the middle of the pool, there is an eight-sided, stalactite-capped pier, with a pipe on each surface. Water is poured into the
pool through the pipes. There are ablution taps in the section where the
shadirvan, the stairwell and the mihrab are located.

On dört basamaklı mermer merdivenle çıkılan mescidin merdiven korkulukları
da mermerden yapılmıştır ve geometrik şebekelidir. Ahşap kapılı mescidin
zemini de ahşap kaplamadır. Diğer altı köşede ise alçı pencereler mevcuttur,
dıştan demir parmaklıklarla kapatılmış pencerelerin üzerlerinde sivri kemerli
bir alınlık vardır, alınlık kısmı beşgenlerden oluşan petekle bezenmiştir.

The stair railings of the masjid, which is accessed by a fourteen-step marble
staircase, are also made of marble and have a geometric grid. The floor of
the masjid with wooden doors is also covered with wood. There are plaster
windows on the other six corners, the windows are closed with iron grilles
on the outside and there is a pointed arched pediment above them, the
pediment part is decorated with a honeycomb formed by pentagons.

Due to the increase in congregations, another wooden masjid was built
next to the masjid by Hoca Mir, but the building has not survived to the
present day.
The most comprehensive restoration of the Kozahan Pavilion Masjid, which
was repaired in 1584, 1627, 1643, 1671 and 1778, was carried out in 1948
by the Bursa Association for the Appreciation of Antiquities with the help
of merchants. According to the records in the Bursa sharia records, it was
understood that the masjid, which was covered with a wooden roof at that
time, was originally a domed structure. For this reason, the masjid was
restored to its domed form using bricks taken from the ruins of the Tahtakale (Bayezid Paşa) Han. This activity was also the first serious work of the

The pavilion masjid located in the courtyard of the Koza Han was restored
by a private organization in 2008. This elegant structure, which was included in the UNESCO World Heritage List in 2014, is an important part
of Bursa’s Historical Bazaar and Hans District.

While the work bears elegant traces of Anatolian Seljuk architecture, it also
stands out as one of the most beautiful examples of the early Ottoman period.
