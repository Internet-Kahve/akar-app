# Eyne Bey (İnebey) Madrasa · Story (English)

Translation of `../hikaye.md`. Sources and trust labels come from the Turkish record.

| # | Sentence (EN) |
|---|---|
| 1 | The İnebey Madrasa was commissioned in the early 15th century by Eyne Bey, governor of Bursa under Yıldırım Bayezid. |
| 2 | With his endowment deed of 1402, Eyne Bey set aside income from baths, mills, vineyards and villages in five towns for the madrasa and a zawiya. |
| 3 | Under the deed, the teacher's daily salary of 10 dirhams was to be paid from the income of the bath in Bursa. |
| 4 | Each of the twelve students staying in twelve rooms was also allotted one and a half dirhams a day. |
| 5 | At the centre of the 8 × 10 metre courtyard is an octagonal fountain with a jet. |
| 6 | The courtyard is surrounded by pointed-arched arcades on twelve columns of küfeki stone. |
| 7 | When madrasas closed in 1924 the building was sold and served as a canning factory until 1962; then the Foundations Administration bought it back. |
| 8 | In 1969 the manuscripts and printed books of the library in Orhan Mosque moved here; the madrasa is now the İnebey Manuscript Library. |
