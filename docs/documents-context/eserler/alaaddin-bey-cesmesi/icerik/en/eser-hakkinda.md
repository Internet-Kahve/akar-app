# Alaaddin Bey Fountain · About (English)

## Konum ve önemi | Location and significance

Alaaddin Bey Mosque is in the Alaaddin Bey neighbourhood of the Osmangazi district.

Considered the oldest fountain in the city, this structure was built against the mosque's courtyard wall, facing the street.

## Mimari | Architecture

With features typical of early Ottoman architecture, the fountain has alternating courses of cut küfeki stone and brick, a pointed-arched niche, a row of saw-tooth brick eaves and a wide front panel, reflecting an original design that represents the first examples of Ottoman city fountains.

Facing the street, outside the courtyard wall, the fountain shows careful alternating masonry. Its body is built of one course of cut küfeki stone and two of brick, with upright bricks set between the courses. On the panel the brick courses rise to three, and upright bricks are used here too.

The wide pointed arch covering nearly the whole front, common in early Bursa fountains, is built of cut white travertine with courses of brick between. The arch gives the front a soft transition, and the row of saw-tooth brick above it strengthens the building's appearance.

Immediately above the arch, which rests on a base about 40 cm high, is a covering crowned by two rows of saw-tooth eaves and laid with Turkish tiles.
